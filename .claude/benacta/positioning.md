# BENACTA — Positioning

> Binding doctrine. Consult before writing any public-facing copy, README section, PDF page or UI label.

## What BENACTA is

BENACTA designs and builds **AI-Native Decision Systems** at the intersection of:

- Enterprise AI
- Decision Intelligence
- Data
- Business Logic
- Workflows
- Human Judgment

Initial domains: Finance, Operations, Performance Management, Planning, Enterprise Workflows.
**Finance is the wedge — BENACTA is broader than Finance.** Never present BENACTA as a finance-only shop; never present this project as the whole of BENACTA.

### Canonical positioning block (use verbatim)

> **BENACTA**
> Enterprise AI · Decision Intelligence
> **AI-Native Decision Systems**
> Turning enterprise data into decisions and action.
> **Built on Truth. Designed for Decisions.**

Author identity on public assets: **Anas Benazzouz — BENACTA · AI Engineering · Finance & Operations**.

## What BENACTA is not

Not an AI chatbot agency, a RAG agency, a generic automation agency, a dashboard consultancy, a Power BI consultancy, an "AI agents everywhere" company, or a ChatGPT-wrapper builder. BENACTA understands hype and uses technologies only when architecture and business value justify them.

## Audience

- **Primary:** CEO, CFO, COO, Finance Director, Transformation Director.
- **Secondary:** CIO, CTO, Enterprise Architect, AI Engineer, Data Engineer, Finance Transformation.

Every deliverable defaults to the primary audience: a CEO or CFO must benefit without looking at code. The default experience must feel like a miniature **Decision System**, not a chatbot.

## Brand character

Premium · editorial · institutional · enterprise · understated · architectural.
Think: strategy publication, architecture blueprint, premium finance institution, enterprise operating system. Private-bank note, not SaaS.

## Success criteria (what each reader must leave thinking)

- **CFO:** "I understand how AI can help Finance without controlling the numbers."
- **CEO:** "I can see how this connects data to decisions and action."
- **CIO:** "The architecture has sensible boundaries."
- **Finance Controller:** "This augments my work rather than removing my accountability."
- **Enterprise Architect:** "The semantic/business-object layer and trust boundary are meaningful."
- **AI Engineer:** "The deterministic and probabilistic responsibilities are separated cleanly."
- **Potential client:** "BENACTA seems to understand enterprise data, Finance, AI architecture and decision workflows."
