# FY26 Forecast Assumptions

Meridian Industrial Group · Group Finance
Fictional document, produced for the BENACTA reference implementation.

## Revenue Recognition Basis

Project revenue is recognised on customer acceptance of contractual milestones.
Revenue is not recognised on progress, on invoicing or on shipment. A milestone
that moves between periods therefore moves the associated revenue with it.

Service contract revenue is recognised evenly across the contract term. Spare
parts and repair revenue is recognised on delivery.

## Budget Phasing

The FY26 budget phases project milestone revenue according to the contractual
milestone plan agreed at the start of the year. It does not carry a general
allowance for milestone slippage between months. Month-to-month timing
differences on milestone revenue are therefore expected and are not treated as
a change to the full-year outlook unless the milestone moves outside the
quarter.

## Contractor Capacity Assumptions

The FY26 plan assumed that automation engineering capacity would be met from
internal resources for the full year. External engineering contractors were
budgeted only for specialist packages. The plan carries no allowance for
covering open internal vacancies with external contractors.

## Procurement Assumptions

The plan assumes material input prices track the index agreed with procurement
at the start of the year. Volume rebates are assumed to be recognised in the
final quarter.

## August 2026 Assumptions

The August forecast includes both milestones rescheduled from July, together
with their associated direct project costs. No further reschedule is assumed.
