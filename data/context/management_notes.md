# Management Commentary — July 2026

Meridian Industrial Group · Business Unit submissions to Group Finance
Fictional document, produced for the BENACTA reference implementation.

## Projects Business Unit

Revenue finished below plan for the month. The shortfall sits almost entirely on
milestone revenue, following the reschedule of two customer acceptance
milestones into August (see the Project Milestone Register). Engineering
services revenue was slightly ahead of plan.

Order intake and backlog are unchanged. No contract was lost, reduced or
renegotiated during the period.

## External Engineering Capacity

Two senior engineers left the automation team in Q1 and the replacement
recruitment is still open. To hold the delivery programme, the business unit
extended two external engineering contracts and added a third contractor in
June. External engineering contractor costs finished approximately €125,000
above plan for the month.

The arrangement is temporary and is being managed through to the close of the
recruitment. The FY26 plan assumed this work would be delivered internally.

## Procurement

Input prices for steel sections and electrical components settled below the
budgeted index for the second consecutive month, and one framework supplier
applied a volume rebate earlier than assumed in the plan. Material costs
finished below plan, which partly protected gross margin despite the revenue
shortfall.

## Service & Maintenance Business Unit

Revenue finished marginally ahead of plan, supported by spare parts demand.
Subcontracted field labour was slightly below plan. No material issues to report
for the period.

## Customer Workshops

Three customer workshops were held during July that were not included in the
annual plan. Two were requested by customers following the milestone reschedule;
the third supported a pre-tender discussion. The workshops drove additional
travel and accommodation costs, and associated logistics costs were recorded
against a newly opened account.
