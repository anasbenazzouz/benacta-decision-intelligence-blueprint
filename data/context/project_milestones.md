# Project Milestone Register FY26

Meridian Industrial Group · Projects Business Unit
Fictional document, produced for the BENACTA reference implementation.

## July 2026 Milestone Status

Two customer acceptance milestones planned for July were rescheduled into
August at the customer's request. Neither reschedule reflects a scope change, a
commercial dispute or a quality issue, and both contracts remain in force.

- **MS-2214 Northgate Water Treatment, Phase 2 acceptance.** Planned 18 July,
  rescheduled to 12 August. The customer's site was unavailable for the
  acceptance test in the planned week. Revenue recognised on milestone
  acceptance: €195,000.
- **MS-2231 Halden Terminal, commissioning acceptance.** Planned 29 July,
  rescheduled to 7 August. The customer requested a combined acceptance with a
  parallel package. Revenue recognised on milestone acceptance: €150,000.

## Milestone Cost Recognition

Direct project costs follow the milestone they belong to. Site installation,
commissioning and equipment rental costs associated with MS-2214 and MS-2231
were deferred into August together with the milestones themselves. The July
underspend on direct project costs is therefore a timing difference and is
expected to reverse.

## August 2026 Outlook

Both rescheduled milestones are confirmed in the August plan and the site dates
are agreed with the customers. Project Finance has not identified a risk to
recognition within the quarter, but confirmation of the August acceptance dates
remains outstanding at the time of the July close.
