# Travel and Customer Engagement Policy

Meridian Industrial Group · Group Finance · FY26
Fictional document, produced for the BENACTA reference implementation.

## Scope

This policy covers travel, accommodation and directly associated logistics costs
incurred by all business units, including costs incurred in connection with
customer visits, workshops and site attendance.

## Budget Ownership

Travel budgets are set annually per cost center and are owned by the cost center
manager. The annual plan phases travel evenly across the year except where a
known event profile justifies otherwise.

## Unplanned Customer Workshops

Customer workshops that are not included in the annual plan require approval
from the business unit director before commitment. Where a workshop is expected
to generate logistics costs that are not covered by an existing budget line, a
budget line must be opened before the costs are incurred, or the costs must be
reallocated to an existing approved line.

Workshops held in response to a customer request during an active project are
frequently agreed at short notice. This is recognised as a legitimate commercial
need, but it does not remove the requirement for a budget line.

## Approval Thresholds

Individual trips above €5,000 require business unit director approval.
Cumulative monthly travel spend above the cost center plan is reported to Group
Finance with an explanation from the cost center manager.
