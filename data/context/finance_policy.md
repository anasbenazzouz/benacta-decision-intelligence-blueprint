# Finance Policy — Variance Reporting, Materiality and Commentary

Meridian Industrial Group · Group Finance · FY26
Fictional document, produced for the BENACTA reference implementation.

## Materiality Thresholds

A variance against approved budget is reported to the controller when it breaches
either threshold below.

- **Absolute materiality: €100,000.** Any variance of €100,000 or more on a
  reported metric is escalated regardless of its relative size.
- **Relative materiality: 10%.** Any variance of 10% or more of the budgeted
  amount is escalated, provided the absolute variance is at least €25,000. The
  floor prevents small-base accounts from generating noise.

Favourable variances are reported on the same thresholds as unfavourable ones. A
favourable movement of material size still requires an explanation: it is
frequently a timing difference rather than a genuine saving, and it may reverse
in a later period.

## Budget Line Governance

Every account carrying actual postings must have an approved budget line for the
period. Where actuals are recorded against an account with no budget line, the
movement cannot be assessed against plan and is reported to the controller as a
governance gap. The budget owner is responsible for opening a budget line or
reallocating the cost before the following close.

## Commentary and Approval

Financial figures published in the monthly performance review are computed from
the general ledger and the approved budget. Narrative commentary may be drafted
with assistance, but it is not published until a qualified controller has
reviewed and approved it. The controller remains accountable for the published
commentary. Requesting a revision is a normal outcome of review, not an
exception.

## Variance Ownership

Each reported variance is assigned an owner in the responsible function. The
owner is accountable for the explanation and for any agreed follow-up action.
Revenue recognition questions are owned by Project Finance. Cost variances are
owned by the budget holder of the cost center concerned.
