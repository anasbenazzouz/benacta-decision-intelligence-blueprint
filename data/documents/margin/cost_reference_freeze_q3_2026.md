## Purpose

Reference unit costs for the third quarter were frozen on 2 June 2026 from the last negotiated supplier prices.
Sales prices and margins for the quarter are assessed against these references.

## Handling of variances

A realised receipt cost above the reference on delivered goods is a purchase price variance. It is not recovered
from the customer. Procurement reviews the supplier price; the reference is updated only at the next freeze, unless
a supplier price change is contractual and documented.

## Missing costs

Goods delivered without a valued receipt cannot be measured. The margin of those lines is unavailable until the
receipt is posted or the cost is attributed; nothing is estimated in the meantime.
