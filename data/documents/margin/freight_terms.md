## Rebill clause

Customers whose contract carries a rebill clause are charged the contractual freight amount once the goods of an
order are fully delivered and fully invoiced. Freight is invoiced on the goods invoice or on a separate freight
invoice referencing the order.

## Waived and included clauses

A waived clause means freight is never charged; an included clause means freight is part of the goods price.
Neither creates a freight receivable.

## Partial deliveries

Freight is not due while any line of the order remains undelivered or uninvoiced. A partial recharge below the
contractual amount is a billing omission unless the account manager recorded an approved reduction.

## Corrections

Freight omitted or under-charged is invoiced on a complementary invoice referencing the original order.
