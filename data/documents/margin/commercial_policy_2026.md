## Discount caps by customer segment

Standard customers may receive up to 5 % discount on list price. Key accounts may receive up to 10 %. Distributors
were covered by a separate policy that expired on 2 June 2026 and has not been renewed; until a new distributor
policy is approved, distributor discounts cannot be assessed and must be reviewed one by one.

## Derogations

A discount above the cap requires a written derogation approved by a finance approver before the order is
confirmed. A derogation names the order, the maximum discount, the approver, the approval date and a validity
window. Derogations are recorded in the derogation register; an order without a matching valid derogation is a
policy exception, whatever the sales context.

## Customer-specific policies

When a customer has its own policy, it takes precedence over the segment policy. Two customer policies of the
same priority with different caps are a governance defect: the sales director resolves it, and the lines stay
under review until then.

## Corrections

An excess discount already invoiced is recovered through a complementary invoice unless a derogation is granted
after the fact; a derogation after invoicing must state that it covers the invoiced order.
