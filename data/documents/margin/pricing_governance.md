## Baseline hierarchy

The expected price of a line is, in order: the contract price agreed with the customer for the product, the price
list assigned to the customer, the product list price, or a comparable recent price when nothing else exists. A line
priced below its baseline without an approved reason is a pricing exception.

## Price lists

Each customer is assigned one price list. An order must apply the customer's price list; applying another list,
for instance a distributor list to a standard customer, is an order entry error and the order is re-priced before
invoicing or corrected by a complementary invoice.

## Contract prices

A contract price is valid for its period and minimum quantity. Invoicing below the contract price without a signed
amendment is a billing error to be recovered.

## Corrections

Pricing exceptions already invoiced are corrected by a complementary invoice or, with the approver's agreement,
recorded as an accepted commercial gesture with a written justification.
